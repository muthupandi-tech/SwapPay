How does matching work?

When you create a swap, the system looks for other users in your campus radius who need the opposite currency (e.g., you have UPI and need Cash). Matches are either one-to-one or "crowd-swaps" where multiple users fulfill a larger request.

What is Trust Score?

Your Trust Score is based on ratings from other users after completed swaps. A higher score makes your requests more visible. If your score drops below 2 stars, you'll need to complete recovery swaps to improve your standing.

Is my money safe?

SwapPay facilitates matches by connecting users physically on campus. Always verify the transaction with your partner in person before marking a swap as completed.

How do I delete a swap?

You can delete an active swap from your "My Swap Activity" tab. Note that once a swap is matched, you must complete it or chat with your partner before it can be resolved.
