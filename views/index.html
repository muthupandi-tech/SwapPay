Hi, I'm Muthu Pandi K, a 3rd year Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) student who is passionate about technology and building useful products.

I didn’t come from a privileged background, but I believe in learning step by step with consistency. For me, development is not just coding — it’s about finding real problems and creating simple solutions for them.

The idea for my project, SwapPay, came from my college life. I noticed that many students struggle to exchange cash and UPI during urgent situations. So I built SwapPay — a platform where students can easily find and exchange money with each other.

Through this journey, I learned how to solve real-world problems, improve user experience, and keep growing as a developer.

My goal is to become a strong software engineer and build products that truly help people in their daily life.
